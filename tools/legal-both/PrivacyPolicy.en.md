## 2. In Short
- SnapTask has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, on any platform.
- What you create in the App stays on your device. On Apple devices it can also be in your private iCloud if you turn on sync; on Android it can be in your Google backup if Android Backup is on. We cannot see it.
- We only receive what you choose to post on the in-App Community board, a notification token if you post there on an Apple device, and any email you send us.

## 3. Data That Stays With You
Everything you create in SnapTask (tasks, categories, notes, photos, voice memos, journal entries, rewards, statistics and settings) is stored on your device.

Apple versions: if you enable iCloud sync, it is also stored in your private iCloud database under your Apple ID, which is managed by Apple. We have no access to that database and cannot read, change or delete it. Shared Spaces (from SnapTask 2.0): if you share a space, its content and your iCloud name are shared through iCloud with the people you invite. We do not receive this data either.

Android: if Android Backup is on, your tasks and settings are saved in your Google account backup and restored on a new phone. Google protects and manages this backup, we cannot access it, and you control it in your phone's Google settings. Photos and voice memos are not saved to the cloud; they only move with a phone-to-phone transfer. Calendar links are not backed up, because calendar identifiers belong to the device.

## 4. Data We Receive
### A. Community board (optional)
If you post a suggestion, reply, vote or like on the Community board, we store on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore):
- the title, text and category of your suggestion and the text of your replies;
- the name you choose to show (optional: you can post anonymously);
- a random identifier created by the App on your device, used to recognise your own posts, votes and likes;
- dates and vote/like counts.

Suggestions and replies are public: everyone who uses SnapTask can read them. Please do not include personal information in them. You can delete your own posts in the App at any time.

### B. Reply notifications
Apple versions: if you post a suggestion, the App also stores on Firebase your device's Apple push notification token, the App language and platform, and the random identifier above, so we can let you know when we reply. Notifications are shown only if you allow them. The token is used for nothing else.

Android: the App does not use push notifications. About once an hour it asks the Community board whether there are replies to your suggestions, using the random identifier above, and shows a notification on your phone if there are. We do not store a device or notification token.

### C. Emails
If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifier, usage analytics or crash reports. If you allowed your device to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash reports and aggregated sales reports with us; they handle them under their own policies.

## 5. Services Your Device Uses Directly
Some features connect your device directly to other providers. We do not receive this data:
- Apple versions: Apple iCloud (CloudKit) for sync and Shared Spaces; Apple App Store (StoreKit) for purchases; Apple speech recognition for voice input (on the device when supported, otherwise by Apple); Apple Calendar and Google Calendar if you connect them; QuoteGarden, from which the App downloads the daily quote (it sees an ordinary internet request, such as your IP address, but no SnapTask data).
- Android: Google Play (Play Billing) for purchases; Android speech recognition for voice input (on the device when supported, otherwise by Google); your phone's calendars, only if you allow calendar access; the Android geocoder for address search, which may contact Google; OpenStreetMap map tiles, whose servers see an ordinary internet request (such as your IP address and the area you view) but no SnapTask data. The daily quote is stored inside the Android App and needs no connection.

Apple and Google handle payments; we only receive aggregated sales reports, never your payment details.

- Legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records for sales (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell data, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Google and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area, with safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## 8. How Long We Keep Data
- Suggestions, replies, votes and likes: until you delete them in the App, ask us to delete them, or we close the board.
- Notification token (Apple versions): while you have suggestions on the board, or until you ask us to delete it.
- Emails: as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up.
- Data on your device, in iCloud or in your Google backup: under your control. Deleting the App removes local data; you can delete iCloud data in iOS Settings, and the Google backup in your Google settings.

## 9. Security
Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit and stored on Google's infrastructure. The Community board is public by design, so treat anything you post there as public.

## 10. Your Rights
Under the GDPR, and similar laws where they apply (such as the UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and LGPD), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Write to the email above. Since there are no accounts, tell us the title of your suggestions so we can find them. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

## 11. Children
SnapTask is not directed to children under 13. Children under the age of digital consent in their country (14 in Italy, up to 16 elsewhere in the EEA) should post on the Community board only with a parent's permission. If you think a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Permissions
Your device asks for your permission before SnapTask uses notifications, calendars, the camera, photos, the microphone, speech recognition, your location (Android and map features) or exact alarms (Android reminders). They are used only for the feature you are using, and you can revoke them in Settings at any time.
